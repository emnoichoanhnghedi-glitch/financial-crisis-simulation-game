# Financial Crisis Simulation Game

## Project Overview
This project is a phase-based financial crisis simulation game developed for the NHA408E Midterm Project. The game allows players to experience key decision-making moments during major financial crises and observe how different portfolio choices affect investment outcomes.

## Selected Cases
The game includes three crisis cases:

1. **Global Financial Crisis 2008–2009**  
   A simulation of the credit market collapse, liquidity stress, and systemic risk during the global financial crisis.

2. **COVID-19 Market Shock**  
   A simulation of the sudden market disruption caused by the pandemic and the policy responses that followed.

3. **Tulip Mania**  
   A historical bubble case illustrating speculation, price escalation, and market collapse.

## Game Structure
The game is designed around a six-phase crisis arc:

1. Early Warning Signals  
2. Bubble Expansion  
3. Market Stress  
4. Crisis Peak  
5. Policy Response  
6. Recovery and Reflection  

At each phase, players review market data, news signals, and decision prompts before selecting a portfolio strategy.

## Learning Objectives
Through this simulation, players are expected to:

- Understand how financial crises develop over time
- Identify warning signals in financial markets
- Analyze the relationship between news, market data, and investor behavior
- Make portfolio allocation decisions under uncertainty
- Reflect on the consequences of risk-taking during crisis periods
